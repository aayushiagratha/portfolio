Job Search Score

A sponsorship-aware job search agent — built to solve my own problem.

View on GitHub ↗

Most job boards can't tell you whether a company can legally sponsor a visa or how contested a role really is. This checks both, then scores the role against my actual CV before I spend an evening writing a cover letter for a role I was never eligible for.

The problem

Most listings answer the wrong question first — what the role involves, before whether the company is even allowed to hire you for it. Under a visa that requires a licensed sponsor and a salary floor, a perfect-fit role at a company without a sponsor licence isn't a long shot. It's not available at all, but it still ranks at the top of a keyword search next to roles that were never real options.

How it works

  • Sponsor check — can this company legally sponsor a Skilled Worker visa.
  • Salary floor — does the role clear the visa's minimum salary requirement.
  • Fit — only then, does this actually match the CV, scored with real ATS-style keyword analysis.

What I found

In my search, the strongest predictor of a rejection wasn't the responsibilities in the job description. It was one word in the title — "Manager" bounced almost every time, while titles like "Analyst" or "Specialist," covering equivalent work, stayed live.

Field note
The word in the title predicted more than the job did →
